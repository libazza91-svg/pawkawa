# Public Copy and Claims Policy

Status: Sprint 3.0 baseline

## Purpose

Pawkawa is currently an AU cat food price-comparison MVP. Public copy must help users understand tracked retailer prices without implying whole-market coverage, medical advice, or guaranteed savings.

## Approved Price Language

- Best price found from tracked retailers
- Lowest listed price from current tracked offers
- Currently tracked retailer offers
- Coverage is limited to connected and validated sources

## Avoided Price Language

- Cheapest in Australia
- Guaranteed lowest price
- Best price everywhere
- Complete market coverage

## Conditional Pricing Rules

- Member prices must be labelled as requiring membership.
- Coupon prices must be labelled as requiring a coupon.
- Minimum-spend discounts must show the minimum spend clearly.
- Conditional prices must not be styled as the guaranteed best available price unless the backend marks them as unconditional.

## Coverage Language Rules

- Show tracked retailer count when possible.
- Use limited-coverage labels for one-retailer or low-data products.
- Do not hide low-coverage states from search or price detail pages.
- Homepage preview sections may show stronger examples, but must not imply full-market completeness.

## Nutrition and Medical Safety Boundary

Pawkawa may show product context, nutrition fields and suitability notes, but it must not provide veterinary treatment advice.

Avoid:

- treats disease
- cures stomach issues
- prevents illness
- recommended for a medical condition

Allowed:

- may be more suitable for sensitive digestion
- consider veterinary guidance
- not a substitute for veterinary advice
- requires vet confirmation

## Public MVP Copy Review Checklist

- The copy clearly says what data is tracked.
- The copy does not overclaim coverage.
- Conditional prices are separate from main comparable price.
- Product Compare is secondary to Find Prices.
- Learn and About explain source limits and confidence methods.
